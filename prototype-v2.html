The closed loop

step 1

Identify

17 GenAI fraud vectors researched across identity, social engineering, transactions, merchants, and the AI stack itself.

step 2

Attack

High-fidelity simulation: per-user behavioral profiles, realistic timing, merchant, geo, and text-field signals.

step 3

Defend

Gradient-boosted detector scored on precision, recall, F1, AUC, and a per-attack breakdown.

Feedback loop: every attack the detector misses is regenerated as a harder variant and fed back into training — the system hardens itself without a human in the loop.
